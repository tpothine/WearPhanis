Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 5-day rental window work? +
Your pieces arrive by Thursday evening (or are picked up locally in Central NJ). You wear them Friday through Sunday for your celebrations. The set must be postmarked back at USPS/UPS or dropped off locally by Monday at 12:00 PM.
Do I need to clean or sanitize the set before returning? +
No! Please do not use water, soap, or ultrasonic cleaners. Liquids damage foil-backed Kundan and Polki. We handle all medical-grade sterilization of earring posts and specialized dry jewelers' cleaning in-house.
When do I receive my security deposit back? +
Once your return is scanned at our NJ studio, we complete an inspection within 48 business hours. Your full deposit is automatically credited back to your original payment method.
What if a small stone comes loose on the dance floor? +
Normal wear and tear is fully covered! If a small stone pops loose, just place it in the box with the piece. We never charge for minor, repairable stone resets.
